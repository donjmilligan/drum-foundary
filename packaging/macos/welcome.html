Drum Foundry @VERSION@

A kick and snare synthesizer by Milligan Audio.

This installer puts the following on your Mac (you can leave any of the first three out under Customize):

  • VST3 plug-in: /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Drum Foundry.vst3
  • Audio Unit plug-in: /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Drum Foundry.component
  • Standalone app: /Applications/Drum Foundry.app
  • Licence texts: /Library/Application Support/Drum Foundry/Licenses
